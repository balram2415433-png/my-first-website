Wolf

Folk culture represents the traditional, heart-driven expressions of everyday people that are passed down through generations. It includes everything from soulful music and traditional dances to oral storytelling, handmade crafts, and unique local festivals. Unlike popular culture, which is often shaped by mass media and commercial industries, folk traditions are born directly from the shared experiences, beliefs, and history of a specific community
